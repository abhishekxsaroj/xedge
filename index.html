Xedge started with a simple problem: brokers were stitching together separate systems for liquidity, execution, and risk, and losing time and revenue in the gaps between them.

Built on technology developed by AegeanLabs, Xedge brings those pieces into one infrastructure, so a broker can see their exposure, route their flow, and manage liquidity from a single place instead of five.

The result: less manual work for dealing teams, fewer blind spots, and a platform that scales from a single-desk startup to a multi-entity institutional operation.
